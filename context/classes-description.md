# Class Description

## Puppy Kindergarten

**$80 (4 weeks)**

In this class, we cover a variety of puppy issues such as mouthing, jumping, crate training, socialization, and house training. In addition, we introduce puppy manners and obedience commands such as "sit", "down" and "come." The course goal is to set your puppy up for success as he or she matures into adulthood and advanced training.

---

## Dog About Town Basic Obedience

**$400 (7 Weeks)**

Classes are designed to have no more than 8 dogs per class and 2 trainers in each class. We want each team to get the attention they deserve in a smaller class while learning "Heel, Sit, Down, Stay, Come, Leave It, Drop It" and the importance of the word "No". Behavioral problems such as mouthing and jumping, as well as others, will be addressed. Throughout the class handlers will learn to deal with everyday situations with their dogs such as running critters, barking dogs, strange surfaces and more. Any training devices needed will be provided. You and your dog will also have the opportunity to take the AKC Canine Good Citizens Test as part of the class.

---

## Intermediate Suburban K9

**$400 (6 Weeks)**

This is the next class after mastering on leash obedience. These classes are 4-6 dog and owner teams to 2 trainers. The class is a 6 week class that teaches you how to work with your dog with greater distractions and maintain control. Also, you and your dog will learn 18 new commands like retrieval, walking backwards, coming when called while other dogs play and much more. Your dog will learn greater self control and you will learn how to help your dog through the unexpected surprises the world throws at us. You and your dog will also have the opportunity to take the AKC Canine Good Citizens Test and the AKC Community Canine Test.

---

## Advanced Country K9

**$400 (5 Weeks)**

After mastering on leash commands it's time to give your K9 partner some freedom. These classes are 4-6 teams to 2 trainers. The class is a 7 week class that teaches you how to work with your dog at a greater distance and maintain control while on a 20ft leash. We will work with distractions and prepare you for the life of long line walks. This class will help you know if you want to take the next step to off leash and still give you and your dog more freedom without losing a well behaved companion. You and your dog will have the opportunity to take the AKC Advanced Canine Good Citizen Test also known as The Community Canine.

---

## Elite Freedom K9 (off leash)

**$400 (6 Weeks)**

You have mastered on leash and distance commands, it's time to set your K9 partner free. These classes are 4-6 teams to 2 trainers. The class is a 7 week class that teaches you how to work with your dog and maintain control while off leash. Don't worry, we will still work with distractions and prepare you for the real world of "Oh look SQUIRREL!" We can teach with E-collars (electric) if you like, but it is not a requirement for the class. (Approved E-collars: Dogtra brands- 200c/ 202c, 200NCPT, ARC, 280c, and 1900s. Petsafe brands: Remote Training Collar 100- 300 yard static, Remote Training Spray, Remote training Vibration.) If you're unsure don't worry we can show you your options so you can make the best decision for you and your dog. You will also have the opportunity to take the AKC Urban Canine Good Citizen Test.

## Service Dog

**$65 per lesson or $100 for group**

Brace/Mobility Support Dogs, Medical Assistance Dogs, Medical Alert, and Psychiatric Service Dogs (PTSD, TBI, and other mental health) Any training devices needed will be provided. Service dogs will be taught/ checked for basic and advanced obedience, they will be taught public access and their needed tasks to help mitigate the handlers disabilities. Time for completion will depend on several factors such as complexity of tasks and how well the team learns together.

## Paws UP Therapy Dog Class

(For All Dogs Age 1 & Up, with Completion of Basic Obedience Class)

**$100 (4-Week)**

This small group class will cover the basics of what therapy dogs and their handlers do during visits to various places such as schools, hospitals, and nursing homes. Dogs and handlers will be taught how to handle the various environments that may be present during visits. Handlers will learn to recognize signs of stress in their dogs, as well as how dogs provide calming effects on people. Preparation for Therapy Dog Evaluation credentialing is included in the course.

## Personal Protection

**$4000**

This class is specifically designed to ensure your dog is still a friendly, social member of the family and not a public liability. Your dog should already have passed Basic Obedience through Off-Leash Obedience as a part of this course. Your dog will be taught to alert to intruders by barking and placing themselves between the threat and their owners. These dogs are NOT trained to ATTACK, but instead to defend their master on command when a threat arises. We will run scenarios to keep you safe during a walk, while in your car, at your home, and more. We are using a PSA dog Sport style obedience and training, so if you are interested in such sports you can compete with your dog as well.
